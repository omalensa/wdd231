Quick Safety Guidelines

  • Always let someone know where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Keep your phone charged and with enough airtime/data.
  • Avoid sharing personal routines publicly on social media.
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, leave the area immediately.
  • Teach children never to open the door or follow strangers.
