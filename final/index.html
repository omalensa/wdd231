The Challenge We Face

Kidnapping remains a serious security concern in many parts of Nigeria, including Kaduna State. Families live in fear, and communities need practical solutions that go beyond traditional security measures.

SafeGuard NG was created to provide education, prevention strategies, and digital tools that help ordinary people stay safer every day.
